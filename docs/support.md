# Support

Smart Download Renamer does not require an account and does not provide paid-only features.

## Product support

Report a bug, request a feature, or ask a privacy question through GitHub Issues:

`https://github.com/tahsinsoyak/smartdownload-renamer-extension/issues`

When reporting a problem, include the extension version, Chrome version, operating system, expected behavior, and what happened. Do not attach private filenames, full local paths, download URLs, or downloaded files unless they have been safely redacted.

## Voluntary developer support

Users who find the extension helpful can voluntarily support continued development on Patreon:

`https://www.patreon.com/tahsinsoyak`

Patreon support is optional. It does not unlock features, change extension behavior, or affect support eligibility.
